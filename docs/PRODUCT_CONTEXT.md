# Product context: Benchside Repair Collective

Benchside is a fictional community workshop. People bring household equipment to a coordinator, who records the problem and assigns it to a volunteer technician. Some repairs need a cost estimate approved by the customer. Some need parts. A repaired item waits for collection before the request is closed.

The workshop's goals are to return useful equipment to people, use limited volunteer time well, and communicate clearly about delays. This is not a sales pipeline or a legal-intake product.

## People

- **Coordinator:** receives items, clarifies priorities, assigns work, manages estimates, and communicates with customers.
- **Technician:** assesses and repairs assigned equipment, records estimates of hands-on effort, tracks parts, and writes notes.
- **Customer:** owns an item and can approve or decline a quoted cost. There is no customer portal in the baseline; the demo lets a coordinator simulate the customer's decision.

## Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Request | One piece of equipment and its repair history. |
| Repair status | The team's manually maintained place in the workflow. |
| Estimate (£) | Proposed cost to the customer. |
| Estimated bench time | Expected hands-on technician minutes; not elapsed time since drop-off. |
| Part | A material or component recorded against a request. |
| Provider accepted | The simulated messaging provider accepted a message. It does not establish delivery or reading. |
| Session | A future block of workshop time. The current app has no session planner. |
